Air con technicians

  • 36 pros

Before you hire anyone

Get the quote in writing. A LINE message is fine. It should say what's included.

Read recent reviews. Look for reviews that mention the type of job you need.

Ask about a warranty. Good tradespeople will come back to fix their own work.

Don't pay everything upfront. For small jobs, pay when the work is done.
